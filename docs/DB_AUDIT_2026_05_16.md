## 8. Cost Estimate for Remaining Gemini Embedding

- Uncovered chunks: 927,259
- Skip candidates: ~687
- **Embeddable chunks: ~926,572**
- Avg chunk: ~210 words median = ~280 tokens
- Total tokens: ~260M tokens
- Gemini price: $0.075/1M tokens
- **Estimated cost: ~$19.50**
- With safety margin (retries, overhead): **~$22**

## 9. Recommendations Before Gemini Launch

1. **No additional pre-filtering needed** — only 687 junk chunks out of 927K (0.07%)
2. **The cleaning layer in the script is sufficient** — handles control chars, unicode, whitespace
3. **Credits-depleted safety stop is in place** — script exits immediately, no retry burn
4. **Monster chunks** — consider re-chunking the 10+ books that are single 100K+ word chunks (future task, not blocking)
5. **Ollama daemon** — keep running alongside Gemini for redundancy. Both write to same table, ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING prevents duplicates.
